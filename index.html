Research work,

Nimbus publishes bounded research notes and artifacts about LEO/NTN systems, agent evaluation, and safe network operations. Each public claim says what was checked, what remains open, and where its limits are.

Why this work

Networked systems increasingly combine uncertain physical conditions with automated decisions. Nimbus studies how evidence, traces, and evaluation gates can make those decisions easier to inspect before they are trusted.
