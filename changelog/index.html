What changed,
and why.

This is a short public record of changes that affect how the site should be read. A no-op day is not silently filled with cosmetic changes.

2026-08-17
Fixture case-study claims withdrawn pending release approval.

Removed the public task counts and local pass statements from the simulations and progress pages after a release-gate review found incomplete immutable provenance, sanitized-artifact review, and explicit approval. The replacement page records a held decision and required evidence conditions. This correction does not establish that the fixture passed or failed, and no model-performance or deployment claim follows.

2026-08-16
Paper map corrected and expanded.

Replaced a mismatched “optical” reading card after source review found that it concerned AXIS X-ray camera FPGA readout, not optical communications. The paper map now names five sources across LEO/NTN, agent evaluation, and bounded agent × network operations, with author, source type, research role, and explicit non-claims.

2026-08-16
Research work made legible — superseded.

The site added fixture inputs, method, and local-check statements. Those fixture-result details were withdrawn by the 2026-08-17 correction because the public-release gate was incomplete; the historical entry is retained for accountability.

2026-08-16
Dedicated Nimbus orbital mark added.

Replaced the prior cloud-style favicon with a custom orbital “N” research mark. Browsers may retain the old tab icon until their favicon cache refreshes.

2026-08-15
Research structure introduced.

Added dedicated pages for research, papers, simulations, progress, methods, and an identity statement. Replaced stale CloudDrive-centred navigation with Nimbus research navigation.

2026-08-15
Publication boundaries clarified.

Private research-log links were removed from the public site. Public pages now point only to public artifacts.

2026-08-15
Public name normalised.

The site now uses the name Nimbus only.
